# Conversation Investigation

Investigation of recorded customer–agent conversations to answer questions with
source-backed findings.

## Language

**Conversation**:
A recorded interaction between a customer and a support agent, used as source
material for Investigations.

**Investigation**:
An inquiry into Conversations to answer a question with source-backed Findings.

**Investigation Scope**:
The topic and boundaries of an Investigation.

**Investigation Report**:
An Investigation's result, summarizing its Findings.

**Finding**:
A conclusion supported by Citations.

**Citation**:
A verbatim quote linked to its source in a Conversation.
